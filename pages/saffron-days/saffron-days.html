Saffron Days

Visuals for a weekend food festival

Saffronأيام الزعفران

This is a concept project created to demonstrate our process. It is not client work.

Overview

A concept visual system for a two-day street-food festival — from posters to social media and wayfinding.

The challenge

The festival needed one look that works in Arabic and English, on a phone screen and on a three-metre banner.

Our approach

  • Built a bilingual type pairing with matched weight
  • Created a flexible pattern from spice shapes
  • Produced templates the organisers can edit themselves

The result

Bold saffron tones, playful shapes, and a layout grid that holds steady in both languages.
