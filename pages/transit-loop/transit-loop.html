Mobile AppsConcept project

Loop

A commute companion for city buses

4 min

This is a concept project created to demonstrate our process. It is not client work.

Overview

A concept app that shows live bus arrivals, crowding, and the easiest route — without the clutter of a full map app.

The challenge

Commuters check transit info in seconds, often while walking. Every extra tap is a missed bus.

Our approach

  • Shadowed commuters on morning routes
  • Designed a glanceable home screen for saved stops
  • Added large, high-contrast arrival times

The result

One-glance arrivals, haptic-style feedback, and a lilac palette that stands out in sunlight.
