Ledgerly

A finance dashboard small teams can read

This is a concept project created to demonstrate our process. It is not client work.

Overview

A concept dashboard that turns invoices, expenses, and cash flow into a view founders can understand in a minute.

The challenge

Most finance tools are built for accountants. Founders need fewer numbers and clearer signals.

Our approach

  • Ran card-sorting sessions to decide what matters first
  • Designed a 'runway' view in plain language
  • Built a design system of 60+ components

The result

Clear hierarchy, honest charts, and a calm palette that keeps attention on what changed.
