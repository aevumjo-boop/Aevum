Halo Market

An ecommerce store for a neighbourhood grocer

This is a concept project created to demonstrate our process. It is not client work.

Overview

A concept online store for an independent grocer that wants to offer same-day delivery without losing its friendly, local feel.

The challenge

Grocery shopping online is repetitive. Customers buy the same twenty items each week, yet most stores make them search from scratch every time.

Our approach

  • Mapped a typical weekly shop from list to doorstep
  • Designed a 'usual basket' that rebuilds last week's order in one tap
  • Built a fast, mobile-first storefront with clear delivery slots

The result

Warm product photography areas, generous tap targets, and a basket that's always one thumb away.
