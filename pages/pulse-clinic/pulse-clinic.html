Mobile AppsConcept project

Pulse

A calm booking app for a family clinic

10:30

This is a concept project created to demonstrate our process. It is not client work.

Overview

A concept mobile app that lets families book appointments, manage prescriptions, and message their clinic in one place.

The challenge

Parents often manage health admin for several people at once. Phone queues and paper slips make simple tasks stressful.

Our approach

  • Interviewed parents about how they juggle family appointments
  • Designed household profiles with one shared calendar
  • Prototyped and tested booking in under 30 seconds

The result

Soft colour coding per family member, large type, and reminders that read like a friendly nudge.
