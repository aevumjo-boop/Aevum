Janan Al-Ordon

An Arabic storefront for Jordanian olive oil, honey, and spices.

jananjo.com

A live client website for Janan Al-Ordon — jananjo.com.

Overview

Janan Al-Ordon needed a calm, Arabic-first website that could carry Jordanian pantry goods — olive oil, honey, herbs, spices, and nuts — from the land to the table.

The challenge

Most food brands online feel generic. The site had to read as Jordanian without becoming a souvenir shop, stay readable in Arabic, and make it easy to browse categories and start a conversation on WhatsApp.

Our approach

  • Designed an Arabic-first layout with a quiet header and a full-bleed hero
  • Grouped products into clear circles — honey, spices, nuts, oils, herbs
  • Kept a persistent WhatsApp path so buyers can ask before they order

The result

A warm, photographic storefront that works on a wide desktop and a phone, with category cards under the hero and a clear next step: explore the products.
